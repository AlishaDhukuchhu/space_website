# Lumera User Journey

## Primary User

A style-conscious shopper looking for refined jewelry for herself or as a gift. She wants to discover pieces quickly, understand the brand feeling, compare options visually, and get help choosing something that matches her taste.

## Journey Overview

### 1. First Impression

**Entry point:** Home page hero.

The shopper lands on Lumera and immediately sees a premium, emotional brand message: "Jewelry That Connects With You." The animated particle background creates a polished first impression and signals that the brand is elegant, personal, and modern.

**User goal:** Decide whether this brand feels trustworthy and worth exploring.

**Current actions:**
- Read hero headline and supporting copy.
- Choose "Shop Collection" or "Explore New Arrivals."
- Scroll down using the visual scroll prompt.

**Opportunity:** The hero CTAs currently appear as buttons but do not navigate to sections. Linking them to `#collections` and `#new-arrivals` would make the first decision feel complete.

### 2. Browse By Category

**Entry point:** "Find Your Signature Piece" section.

The shopper scans category cards for Rings, Necklaces, Earrings, and Bracelets. This supports users who already know the kind of jewelry they want.

**User goal:** Narrow the catalog by product type.

**Current actions:**
- View large category imagery.
- Hover or focus on category cards.
- See an "Explore" cue for each category.

**Opportunity:** Category cards should be clickable and lead to filtered product views or anchored sections.

### 3. Discover New Arrivals

**Entry point:** "New Arrivals" horizontal scroll section.

The shopper browses product cards with names, prices, badges, wishlist controls, hover imagery, and quick-add actions. This is the first strong product-shopping moment.

**User goal:** Find an appealing product and decide whether to save, quick-add, or inspect it.

**Current actions:**
- Scroll through new products.
- Hover to reveal alternate imagery.
- Use wishlist icon.
- Use "Quick Add."

**Opportunity:** Wishlist and Quick Add controls currently have no state or cart behavior. Adding feedback like "Added" or opening size/options would make the interaction feel real.

### 4. Connect With The Brand

**Entry point:** "Designed With Intention" parallax story section.

The shopper pauses from product browsing and gets a brand story moment about craft, longevity, and emotional meaning.

**User goal:** Understand why Lumera pieces are special.

**Current actions:**
- Read the brand message.
- Click "Our Story."

**Opportunity:** Link "Our Story" to an About section or a dedicated brand story page.

### 5. Explore A Featured Collection

**Entry point:** "Made to Be Remembered" featured collection section.

The shopper sees a curated collection with lifestyle imagery and a focused call to action.

**User goal:** Move from broad browsing into a curated collection.

**Current actions:**
- Read collection promise.
- View lifestyle image.
- Click "Explore the Collection."

**Opportunity:** The CTA should route to a collection detail or filtered collection grid.

### 6. Get Personalized Guidance

**Entry point:** "Find the Piece That Feels Like You" quiz.

The shopper answers a short style quiz covering style, jewelry type, metal, and occasion. The app returns a recommended product.

**User goal:** Get help choosing when browsing feels too open-ended.

**Current actions:**
- Select style: Minimal, Classic, Bold, or Romantic.
- Select jewelry type.
- Select preferred metal.
- Select occasion.
- View recommended match.
- Start over if needed.

**Opportunity:** The recommendation currently uses one generic product pattern. It could map answers to real catalog items and provide multiple matches.

### 7. Social Proof And Inspiration

**Entry point:** "Wear It Your Way" Instagram gallery.

The shopper sees lifestyle and product images that suggest how pieces look in real life.

**User goal:** Validate style, imagine wearing the jewelry, and feel social confidence.

**Current actions:**
- Browse image grid.
- Click social-style image links.

**Opportunity:** Images could open a product tag overlay or link to Instagram posts/product pages.

### 8. Footer Conversion

**Entry point:** Footer.

The shopper reaches utility navigation, brand links, customer care, social links, and newsletter signup.

**User goal:** Get reassurance, find support information, or stay connected.

**Current actions:**
- Browse shop, about, customer care, and social links.
- Enter email for newsletter signup.

**Opportunity:** Newsletter form should validate email and show a confirmation state.

## Ideal End-To-End Flow

1. Shopper lands on the hero and feels the brand is premium.
2. She clicks "Explore New Arrivals" or scrolls.
3. She browses product cards and saves a favorite.
4. She uses Quick Add or opens a product detail page.
5. If undecided, she uses the style quiz.
6. The quiz recommends matching products from the catalog.
7. She adds a product to bag.
8. She reviews the shopping bag.
9. She checks out or signs up for the newsletter if not ready to buy.

## Key Missing Screens For A Complete Commerce Journey

- Product listing page with filters and sorting.
- Product detail page with image gallery, materials, sizing, care, and reviews.
- Wishlist page or saved-items drawer.
- Shopping bag drawer or cart page.
- Checkout flow.
- Account sign-in and order history.
- Search overlay with results.
- Confirmation states for newsletter, wishlist, and quick add.

## Recommended Next Improvements

1. Wire all current CTAs to real destinations or anchored sections.
2. Add click behavior and state to Wishlist and Quick Add.
3. Build a product detail page for one product.
4. Connect quiz answers to real product recommendations.
5. Add cart drawer and mini checkout path.
